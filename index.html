The honest spec

What LcinoEditor deliberately does not do

  1. 01
    No VFX compositing depth.LcinoEditor covers the motion graphics creators actually ship — titles, kinetic scenes, animated stats. It is not a node-based AE-class effects environment.
  2. 02
    Mac only, for now.The app is Apple Silicon native. Windows and iPad versions are on the roadmap, not in the box.
  3. 03
    Single-machine, not cloud-collab.Projects live in local .lcpro bundles. There is no shared-team cloud workspace — that is the privacy trade you are making.
  4. 04
    Public content only in the downloader.Private or login-walled posts are refused cleanly. Use it for your own media and public references.

If you need node-based VFX or cloud team review, keep your Adobe seat. If you ship social video for clients or your own channels, LcinoEditor replaces the stack.

Answers

Questions people ask before switching

Is LcinoEditor a good After Effects alternative?

For social and marketing video — yes. LcinoEditor covers the motion-graphics work most creators use AE for (130+ animated scenes, kinetic captions, transitions, color) from one timeline. It intentionally skips AE's deep VFX compositing, and it costs about half as much per month.

Does LcinoEditor work offline?

Completely. Editing, AI clip scoring, Whisper transcription and rendering all run locally on Apple Silicon. Your footage never leaves the machine and no account is required.

What Mac do I need?

macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon (M1–M4). A base M1 MacBook Air renders comfortably; heavier multi-cam work simply finishes faster on newer chips.

How is it half the price of Adobe — what's the catch?

No catch, just no cloud bill. Adobe's price funds server infrastructure; LcinoEditor's AI runs on the chip you already own. Pro is $11.50/month or a one-time $490 Lifetime license with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Can it download YouTube videos and extract MP3?

Yes — YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X, plus a Direct Link mode for any MP4/WebM file such as WordPress media. MP3 extraction and offline transcripts are built in. Public content only.

Is there a free version?

The app installs free from the download and runs fully functional for evaluation, so you can verify every claim on this page before paying anything.

Does my footage or client work leave my computer?

Never. There is no upload step anywhere in the app — that is the core design decision, and the reason the AI features are unmetered.

What happens to my projects if I cancel?

Projects are plain .lcpro bundles in your own Finder folders — open, backed-up and portable. Cancel and every timeline you built stays yours.
